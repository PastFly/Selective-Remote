SelectiveRemote includes the SDL-FreeRDP client and its recursively linked runtime
libraries when a distributable application is built.

FreeRDP is licensed under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/FreeRDP/FreeRDP/blob/master/LICENSE

The SelectiveRemote source distribution also contains the RDPECAM client sources
from FreeRDP 3.30.0 under Native/Camera/FreeRDP. They retain their original
copyright and Apache-2.0 license headers.

SDL is licensed under the zlib license:
https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/main/LICENSE.txt

The build script copies license and notice files installed by Homebrew for each
bundled formula into SelectiveRemote.app/Contents/Resources/ThirdPartyLicenses.
Redistributors are responsible for reviewing those files and satisfying the
licenses of the exact dependency versions in their build.

xterm.js 6.0.0 and @xterm/addon-fit 0.11.0 are bundled as local terminal
resources. They are licensed under the MIT License. The complete license texts
are included in SelectiveRemote.app/Contents/Resources/TerminalResources.
